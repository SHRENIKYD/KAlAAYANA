# Search launch checklist

Metadata uses descriptive page titles, descriptions, per-page canonical URLs,
Open Graph/Twitter previews and Organization JSON-LD with supplied business details.
Google does not use meta keywords; no keyword stuffing or ranking promises.

The current production holding page is the only indexable URL and the only sitemap
entry. Portfolio/staging pages deliberately remain noindex until launch approval.
Do not submit staging domains or remove these guards during the production freeze.

At approved launch: choose the public homepage, enable indexing for intended public
portfolio routes, generate a sitemap containing only canonical indexable URLs,
verify redirects and robots directives, and validate structured data. Verify the
production domain in Google Search Console and submit its sitemap. Request indexing
for the homepage; monitor indexing, search queries and performance. Keep Google
Business Profile contact details and website consistent with the studio, and seek
legitimate client links. These account actions require the owner's connected access.

Reference: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide
