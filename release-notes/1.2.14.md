# v1.2.14 ? Collectables and consistent gallery layouts

Prepared 4 November 2026. Staging candidate only; not published or deployed.

## Collectables
A new flat image gallery sits before Other Installations in homepage Works.
Rounded masonry cards include four Hanuman views, seated sculptures, Karnataka
Mathe details and six framed reliefs. Supplied replacement views are used;
duplicate seated comparison images are removed. White presentation borders are
cropped without clipping the artwork. Six framed screenshots were AI-enhanced
at approximately twice their source dimensions; this reconstructs detail and
must not be described as original high-resolution documentary photography.

## Galleries and navigation
Project links continue opening their individual galleries. Images there now
open the shared preview dialog used by Collectables, with thumbnails, previous
and next navigation, Escape, keyboard support and restored focus on close.
Natural-height masonry avoids stretched imagery. One/two-image galleries are
larger and centered; four-project indexes use two columns on desktop.
Homepage Works reads left-to-right in row order with a full-framed Hanuman cover
and finished Shankaracharya image for Murals. Shared gutters align page content.

## Studio Journal and polish
Refined story/Expertise alignment and Journal spacing. Mobile retains the portrait
position, uses desktop-style copy without a drop cap, and has plain side arrows
plus three navigation dots. Vinay's asset is renamed artist-vinay-kumar.png.
The global circle cursor is hidden outside Works hover; the View indicator remains.
