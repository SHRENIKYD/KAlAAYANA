# v1.2.13 — Studio Journal, artwork catalogue and policy updates

Prepared 2 November 2026. Staging candidate; not a production release.

## Artist profiles

The About area now presents three artists in a wide Studio Journal card instead
of three successive full sections. Each slide pairs a colour portrait with the
user-supplied biography. Desktop uses an editorial two-column layout; mobile
wraps the text beside the portrait in the journal style. The profiles cover:

- Shilpi K. Narayana Rao — Traditional Sculptor | Kannada Rajyotsava Awardee.
- Vishal Kavatekar — Artist and Teacher, with his academic, Academy and
  co-founder background supplied by the studio.
- Vinay Kumar S. — Visual artist and co-founder, including the supplied film
  production design experience. This corrects the earlier Nayi Kumar label.

Previous/next arrows sit at the center of the two card edges. Navigation supports
touch swipes and keyboard arrows, announces the active artist, and excludes
inactive content from interaction. Transitions respect reduced motion and there
is no automatic rotation or visible slide count. Biographical claims are studio
provided; this change does not independently certify awards or appointments.

Narayana Rao's portrait has a transparent background and AI-completed lateral
shoulder/shirt areas. Those areas are reconstructed, not documentary source
pixels. The original photograph and earlier cutout are retained in the repo.
The original ArtistProfile component remains separate from the carousel wrapper
so the layout can be reverted without losing the biographies or source assets.

## Artwork and navigation

Added Hanuman Mural and Sri Adi Shankaracharya mural projects. Shankaracharya's
supplied photograph becomes the homepage Murals cover; its second presentation
image leads the project gallery. Surya uses the newly supplied finished image,
and the homepage Monumental cover uses Hanuman with a corrected crown crop.

Available finished-work images now lead the relevant mural and installation
cards/galleries, including Saraswathi and Siddaramaiah. Village Scenes uses its
third source image as requested. The Sandwich catalogue no longer includes a
contact sheet of unrelated work. Original assets are retained.

Collection navigation includes Home, Works and Contact. Homepage story spacing
is reduced, the duplicate small Our Story label removed, Expertise vertically
centered, and the team sign-off brought closer to the copy. Shared footer layout
keeps short-page footers at the viewport bottom without a fixed content overlay.

## Policies

Footer links open policy content in accessible native dialogs; standalone pages
