# v1.2.15 ? Client identities and artwork framing

Prepared 5 November 2026. Unreleased staging candidate.

## Client showcase
Added 17 client entries in the existing cream/ink theme, with available logos
and text fallbacks. Two synchronized marquee layers progressively reveal colour
near the centre and fade it towards the edges. Hover and keyboard focus pause
movement; individual logo names appear on hover/focus. Reduced-motion users get
a stationary, horizontally scrollable strip. The heading is centred and no
pause button is shown.

Supplied identities include Bengaluru Airport, Habitart, Indian Air Force,
Cauvery Janma Bhoomi Trust, Brahma Brews, Sublime Concepts, Sankara Eye Hospital,
Shri Kanchi Kamakoti Peetham and Chaaru Art Gallery. Corrected the gallery name
and removed duplicate Guruparampara. Selected logos use circular presentation;
Airport and Sublime use transparent backgrounds. Source details are recorded
in docs/CLIENT_LOGOS.md. AI enlargement/background edits do not establish logo
ownership or recover original vector detail.

## Artwork and layout

photographs using AI perspective edits. Original JPGs remain available; edited
PNG assets are supported by the image loader. Removed two unwanted Madhvacharya
views from catalogue data, retaining their source files. The remaining two-image
gallery preserves proportions and rounded corners with existing preview controls.
