## 2. Information involved

An enquiry may include your name, email address, selected project category, message, and any contact details or project references you choose to provide later. A quotation or accepted commission may require billing, delivery, site-access or authorised client-contact information, communicated separately.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card data, Aadhaar copies, health records or unrelated sensitive information. If a project genuinely requires special information, we will explain why and agree an appropriate collection method first.

Website hosting and security providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, request times, requested pages, browser information and security events. This is separate from the enquiry form. We do not claim that visiting the website creates no logs.

## 3. How the enquiry form works

The current form prepares a message in your email application using a `mailto:` link. There is no website enquiry database or server-side form submission endpoint in the reviewed implementation. Pressing the button does not itself confirm delivery to us: you must send the message from your email application.

The form currently has no code that intentionally transmits the entered message to a website form-processing service. Your device, browser and email provider may nevertheless process drafts under their own settings and policies. Once you send an email, our mailbox receives the message and associated email metadata.

## 4. Why information is used

We use enquiry information to respond, understand a project, prepare requested quotations, arrange discussions and, if a commission is accepted, administer that engagement. We also use information where reasonably necessary for security, recordkeeping, responding to complaints and compliance with applicable law.

An enquiry is not permission to subscribe you to unrelated marketing. Any future optional marketing must have a separate, clear choice and an easy way to stop it. We do not sell enquiry information or use it for unrelated targeted advertising.

Submitting an enquiry is voluntary. You may omit optional details or contact us by phone. Without a usable reply address or enough project information, we may be unable to respond meaningfully. This policy is a notice, not a blanket authorisation for every future use of your information.

## 5. Website technologies and third-party services

The website sets no cookies or browser-storage values of its own and carries no advertising trackers. It may use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-first measurement service that counts page views without cookies, fingerprinting or cross-site tracking; the only information processed is the page requested and ordinary technical request data, used in aggregate to understand which pages are read.

Fonts are requested from Google Fonts. The contact page embeds Google Maps. Loading these resources can disclose technical request information to Google, including IP address and browser information; Google may use its own storage or cookies according to its services and your settings. The embedded map can load when the visitor reaches the map area, before the visitor clicks it. We therefore do not describe the website as “cookie-free” or say that third parties receive data only after an outbound click.

Instagram and map-direction links take you to independently operated services. Their handling of information is governed by their own notices. You can restrict third-party resources or storage through browser controls, although fonts or maps may then not display. Read [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Instagram's privacy information](https://privacycenter.instagram.com/policy/).

If analytics, a submission service, uploads, payments or other tracking is introduced, this notice and any necessary choices must be updated before that feature starts operating.

## 6. Sharing and service providers

Information may be handled by our authorised personnel and providers supporting email, hosting, security and project administration. Project information is shared with fabricators, transporters, installers or advisers only to the extent necessary for the relevant task. We do not make your contact details or private references public merely because you contacted us.

We may disclose information where applicable law, a valid legal process or the protection of lawful rights requires it. Requests must be evaluated rather than treated as unlimited permission to disclose. Providers may operate outside India; we do not promise India-only storage. Where Indian law restricts a transfer, the restriction must be respected.

## 7. Retention and security

We keep identifiable information only for the purpose for which it is needed, applicable recordkeeping duties and justified dispute or security needs. An unsuccessful enquiry must not automatically become a permanent customer record. Requests for deletion are considered against the actual records and any lawful need to retain a limited part.

Where a legal hold or mandatory recordkeeping rule applies, we retain the required material for that purpose and restrict unrelated use. Deletion from active systems and expiry from protected backups may occur at different times. We will explain material limits rather than promising immediate deletion from every backup.

We use safeguards appropriate to the information and the risk. These must include controlled mailbox access and sensible account and device security. No internet or email system is completely secure, and this statement is not a claim of a certification or audit. If an incident occurs, we will investigate and provide notices to affected people and authorities where and when applicable law requires.

## 8. Requests, corrections and complaints

Write to contact@kalaayanastudios.com with “Privacy request” in the subject. You can ask about information you provided, request correction or deletion, object to an unrelated use, or withdraw a voluntary permission. If withdrawing information affects an active commission, we will explain the practical consequence and any records still required by law.

We may request proportionate verification before releasing or changing information. We will not routinely demand an Aadhaar copy merely to answer a request. An authorised representative may be asked to demonstrate authority.

We will address privacy grievances promptly and within the applicable legal period; where the 2011 Sensitive Personal Data Rules apply, their grievance-redressal period is one month. If we cannot grant a request in full, we will explain the reason and any available escalation. Rights and complaint routes created by later-commencing provisions apply when those provisions are in force; this policy does not claim that all DPDP remedies were already operative on its preparation date.

## 9. Children and photographs of other people

This website is intended for adult commission enquiries. If you are under 18, ask a parent or lawful guardian to make the enquiry. We do not intentionally seek children's information for marketing or behavioural tracking. If a project includes a child's likeness or another person's personal material, appropriate authority and any legally required consent must be established before that material is used or publicised.

## 10. Changes

We will state the effective date of a revised policy and give appropriate notice of material changes. A new notice does not retrospectively authorise an unrelated use of information already supplied. Any additional consent legally required for a new use must be obtained separately.
