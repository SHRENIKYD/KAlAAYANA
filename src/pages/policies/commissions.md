# Commission, Cancellation and Refund Policy

## 1. Enquiry-only website

Kalaayana's website does not take online orders, deposits or payments. Sending an enquiry does not reserve production capacity or create an obligation to buy. Any commercial engagement is agreed separately in writing. This policy is a framework for that process, not a substitute for a signed quotation or agreement.

## 2. Written scope before payment

Before a client pays, the studio and client must agree the deliverable, material, dimensions, finish, approved references, design stages, included revisions, price, applicable taxes, payment stages and expected schedule. The agreement must allocate packing, freight, insurance, unloading, installation, foundations, permissions and site-readiness responsibilities where relevant.

For a portrait, confirm the identity and authorised references, what constitutes likeness approval, and the stage after which a change requires new work. Record intellectual-property rights, permissions for publicity, and any confidentiality requirements expressly. Do not infer consent to publicise a private commission from payment alone.

## 3. Changes, approvals and delays

Record approvals and requested changes in writing. A material change in scope, price or time requires agreement before the changed work proceeds. The studio must explain the effect of a proposed change rather than imposing an undisclosed charge afterward.

Schedules must reflect actual fabrication and site needs. If a material delay arises, notify the client with the reason, revised proposal and available options. Force-majeure wording must not become an unlimited right to retain payment while never performing; any cancellation or adjustment must respect the agreement and applicable law.

## 4. Cancellation and refunds

There is no blanket “all payments are non-refundable” rule. An enquiry without an accepted paid engagement has no cancellation charge.

Where a client cancels an accepted custom commission, any deduction must follow the agreed lawful terms and reflect justified work already performed or documented, non-recoverable project commitments, taking account of reusable material, recoveries and applicable law. Avoid double recovery, undisclosed penalties and disproportionate forfeiture. Provide an itemised calculation and return any balance due within an agreed, reasonable period.

Where the studio cancels or cannot supply the agreed work, promptly explain the position and agree the refund or other lawful remedy. Do not substitute an unwanted credit note for a refund that is legally due. Statutory rights relating to deficient services, misleading representations, defects or non-conforming work remain available even for custom work.

Every accepted commission must specify refund processing arrangements and the agreed timeframe before payment. No universal timeline, deposit percentage or cancellation percentage is invented by this website policy.

## 5. Delivery, defects and remedies

Agree delivery, inspection and acceptance arrangements appropriate to the work. Record visible transit damage promptly with photographs where possible, but do not use an arbitrary short reporting window to extinguish a legally valid claim or a latent-defect remedy. Notify the studio of a concern as soon as reasonably practicable.

The studio must assess a reported defect or mismatch against the agreed specification and applicable law. Repair, replacement, price adjustment, refund or other relief depends on the facts and legal entitlement, not solely on the studio's unrestricted discretion. Material samples, maintenance instructions and genuine handcrafted variation should be explained before approval; “handmade” is not a defence for failing an agreed specification.

## 6. How to contact us

Email contact@kalaayanastudios.com with the client's name, quotation or invoice reference, relevant dates, issue and requested outcome. Do not send unnecessary identity or payment credentials. These complaint arrangements do not remove access to a consumer commission, court or other competent authority.
