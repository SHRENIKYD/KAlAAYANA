Our Story

A legacy spanning five generations

Embarking upon a legacy spanning five generations, our family lineage is steeped in artistic brilliance. Commencing in the 19th century, our esteemed ancestor, Sri Tippaji, emerged as a luminary in the realm of Mysore style painting. His magnum opus, “Vishwaroopa Darshana,” stands as an epitome of excellence within the traditional Mysore painting genre, earning him the distinction of a Gold Medal recipient from the British government.

Continuing this rich heritage, great grandson of Sri Tippaji, Shilpi K. Narayana Rao, distinguished himself through exceptional contributions to traditional temple architecture and the crafting of monumental cement sculptures, gracing diverse landscapes across India.

Carrying the torch of inspiration ignited by these revered masters, Vishal Kavatekar, alongside a devoted team of artists, founded Kalaayana Studios. Here, the confluence of tradition and innovation unfolds, embodying a commitment to artistic excellence and a profound connection with our storied artistic legacy.
